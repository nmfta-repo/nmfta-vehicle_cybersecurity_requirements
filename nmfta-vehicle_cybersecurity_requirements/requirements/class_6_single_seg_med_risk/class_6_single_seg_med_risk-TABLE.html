        This document specifies cybersecurity requirements for Class 6 vehicle components: Single Segment Medium Risk.

        Class 6 devices are truck modules connected to a single vehicle network segment found to have a medium overall fleet risk.

        Examples include: - Suspension - Steer Axle / ECS / ECAS (J1939 SA 20) - Suspension - System Controller #1 (J1939 SA 47) - Automated Driving (L0-L2) / Forward Looking Camera / Radar (Bendix FLR/FLC) - Engine Valve Controller (J1939 SA 00) - Clutch/Converter Unit (J1939 SA 78) - Auxiliary Transmission (J1939 SA 16) - Collision Avoidance (J1939 SA 42) - Body Controller (Key-Lock Options) - Shift Console Primary / Gearshift ECU (J1939 SA 05) - Safety Restraint System / SRS (J1939 SA 83) - Vehicle Dynamic Stability Controller (J1939 SA 62) - Pyrometer - Adaptive Front Lighting System (J1939 SA 71)
